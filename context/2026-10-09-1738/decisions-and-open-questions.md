# Decisions and open questions

User requested a specification following the recommended face-only display plus
continuous person presence. The spec adopts that interpretation; head/body boxes
are outside scope. Face threshold stays 70%. Provisional schedules/durations are
explicit development proposals, not approved production objectives.

Open: model/preprocessing qualification, numeric product targets, hardware,
tracking algorithm evidence, hung-inference recovery boundary, Firefox and
10/20-camera qualification. No recognition or unique-person identity claim.
